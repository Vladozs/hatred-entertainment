Privacy Policy

Eternal Hatred • HATRED Entertainment

Effective Date: 11.05.2026

HATRED Entertainment ("we," "us," or "our") develops and publishes Eternal Hatred (the "Game"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you play the Game on Steam.

1 Information We Collect

Because the Game is primarily an offline experience, we do not host private servers to collect your personal identification. However, the Game uses third-party services that may collect certain data:

  • Steamworks (Valve Corporation): The Game uses the Steamworks SDK to provide features such as Steam Achievements and Global Leaderboards. This involves processing your Steam ID and Game Statistics (e.g., scores, completion time, kill counts).
  • Discord Rich Presence: If you have the Discord client running, the Game may send basic status information (e.g., "Main Menu," "In-Game — Level 1") to your Discord client to display it on your profile. We do not collect or store your Discord credentials.
  • Technical Data: We may receive anonymized crash reports or hardware specifications through Steam's built-in developer tools to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

2 How We Use the Data

We use the information provided by Steamworks and Discord to:

  • Synchronize and display your in-game achievements and leaderboard rankings.
  • Allow you to share your current game status with friends via Discord.
  • Identify and fix technical issues or gameplay imbalances.

3 Third-Party Services

The Game integrates with the following services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • Valve (Steam): https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/
  • Discord: https://discord.com/privacy

4 Data Storage and Retention

We do not store your personal data on our own servers. Your game progress, achievements, and leaderboard scores are stored and managed by Valve Corporation via the Steam platform. For information on how long Valve retains your data, please refer to their Privacy Policy.

5 Your Rights and Choices

  • Achievements & Leaderboards: You can manage the visibility of your game activity and profile through your Steam Privacy Settings.
  • Discord Status: You can disable "Display currently running game as a status message" in your Discord User Settings under "Activity Privacy."
  • Data Access: You may request access to or deletion of your data directly through Steam Support.
