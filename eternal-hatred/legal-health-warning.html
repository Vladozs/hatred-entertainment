Health & Content Warning

Eternal Hatred • HATRED Entertainment

Read Carefully Before Playing

Photosensitivity / Epilepsy Warning

A very small percentage of individuals may experience epileptic seizures or blackouts when exposed to certain light patterns or flashing lights. Eternal Hatred features intense visual effects, including frequent high-contrast flashes during parrying, combat tricks, and explosions.

If you have an epileptic condition or have had seizures of any kind, consult your physician before playing. Immediately stop playing if you experience dizziness, altered vision, or any involuntary movement.

Content Warning

This game explores extremely heavy and sensitive themes, including:

  • Domestic violence and child abuse.
  • Alcoholism and its consequences.
  • Psychological trauma, coma, and depression.

The narrative depicts the internal struggle of a victim dealing with the physical and emotional aftermath of violence. Player discretion is strongly advised.

Motion Sickness & Well-Being

The fast-paced nature of the gameplay may cause motion sickness or eye strain. We strongly recommend the following:

Recommendations for comfortable play:
  • Take a 15-minute break every hour.
  • Play in a well-lit environment.
  • Adjust "Camera Shake" or visual settings in the options menu if you feel discomfort.
